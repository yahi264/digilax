01

The challenge

The company sells worldwide: hotels in Europe, distributors in the Middle East, buyers in Russia. Their old tools — PDF catalogs sent one by one — did not let buyers browse, compare and ask for a price on their own.

02

What we built

A fast, elegant site that presents hundreds of products by category, in each buyer's language — including right-to-left Arabic. Every product page leads to a clear quote request.

03

The systems behind it

  • Quote form → PHP script on cPanel → email to the company inbox, plus a confirmation to the client in their language
  • WhatsApp, Instagram & LinkedIn buttons on every page
  • Dark/light mode, smooth scrolling and animations
  • QR codes and business cards linked to the site
  • cPanel hosting, domain and SSL
