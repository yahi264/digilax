    07Questions

    Good questions, straight answers.

    Still unsure? Send us a WhatsApp message — we answer quickly.

    How long does a website take?

    A one-page site takes about a week. A complete business site usually takes two to three weeks, depending on how quickly we receive your texts and photos.

    How much does a website cost?

    It depends on what your business needs — number of pages, languages, catalog, forms and hosting. After a free call we send you a clear written quote, and nothing starts until you agree.

    What do I need to prepare?

    Your logo (or we design one), a few photos and a short description of your business. We help with everything else — including writing and translating the texts.

    Can the form send requests to my email?

    Yes. On your hosting, the form emails every request to your address and sends your client a confirmation. You can also receive requests on WhatsApp.

    Do you handle hosting, domain and email?

    Yes. We can set up cPanel hosting, your domain name, the SSL certificate (the padlock) and professional email addresses for your team.

    Can you build in Arabic and other languages?

    Yes — English, French, Turkish, Russian, Arabic and more. Arabic pages are fully mirrored right-to-left, as you can try on this site.

    What happens after launch?

    Every package includes free changes for a period after launch. After that, we offer a simple care plan or you pay only for the changes you need.
